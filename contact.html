Contact Tasbih Digital Counter

Use this page to contact the website team about the counter, a content correction, a source reference, accessibility, or a business enquiry. We especially welcome careful feedback about Arabic text, transliteration, translations and hadith citations.

What to include in a correction request

  • The URL of the page where you noticed the issue.
  • The sentence, translation or reference that needs checking.
  • A reliable source link or book reference, if available.

Feedback and corrections

For feedback, accessibility reports, feature requests or suggested content corrections, open an issue on the project's public GitHub page.

Open GitHub Issues

GitHub issues are public. Do not include private contact details, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information. A GitHub account may be required to post.

Response and privacy

We aim to review genuine messages and reply when a response is needed, but response times may vary. Please do not post passwords, payment details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information in a public issue. A message does not guarantee that a suggested change will be made; religious content updates are checked against relevant sources.

For information about data handled by the website, read our Privacy Policy. For the purpose and limits of the counter and its educational content, see our Disclaimer.
