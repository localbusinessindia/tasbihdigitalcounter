Add the Tasbih Counter to Your Home Screen

Installing the counter puts an icon on your phone or computer and opens it without the browser bars. It is free and takes less than a minute.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the counter in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.

Menu names can change between iOS versions. If you do not see the option, scroll down the Share sheet.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the counter in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen or app drawer.

Windows, Mac and Chromebook (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the counter in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose Install.
  3. Confirm. The counter opens in its own window.

Offline use

After your first visit, the pages and counter are stored by your browser, so the counter can work without internet. Audio from your device’s voice and the pages you have already opened work offline; new pages you have not visited do not.

Important: counts may be stored separately

On some devices the installed icon and the browser keep separate storage, so counts you saved in the browser may not appear in the installed version. To move your counts, open the browser version, choose Export backup, then open the installed version and choose Import backup.

Keep a backup

Browsers can clear site data if you clear history or if storage runs low. Export a backup occasionally and keep the file somewhere safe.

Frequently asked questions

Does installing cost anything?

No. It adds a shortcut to the same free website.

Will it update automatically?

Yes. When you are online the installed version checks for a newer version and loads it the next time you open it.
