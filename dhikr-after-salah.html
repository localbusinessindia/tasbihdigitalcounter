What to say after the salam

A narration from Thawban says that when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ finished prayer, he sought forgiveness three times and then said: Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam, tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram. See Sahih Muslim 591.

The 33-33-34 tasbih after salah

This 33-33-34 sequence is reported in Sahih Muslim 596a. It totals 100 repetitions. The 34 count belongs to Allahu Akbar in this post-prayer narration.

Another authentic way to complete 100

There is also a narration in Sahih Muslim 597a that mentions saying SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times and Allahu Akbar 33 times, then completing 100 with La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah.... These are distinct narrated wordings. This page’s guided counter follows the 33-33-34 version from Sahih Muslim 596a.

Do not confuse the post-salah and bedtime routines

The post-salah version begins with SubhanAllah 33, then Alhamdulillah 33, then Allahu Akbar 34. A separate bedtime narration starts with Allahu Akbar 34, SubhanAllah 33 and Alhamdulillah 33. Read the bedtime dhikr guide for that separate sequence.

Zikr after namaz: a simple sequence to learn

After completing the obligatory prayer and saying the salam, many Muslims continue with istighfar and other forms of dhikr reported in hadith. A familiar routine includes asking Allah for forgiveness three times, saying the supplication beginning “Allahumma antas-salam,” and then reciting tasbih, tahmid and takbir. The exact wording and order should be learned from a reliable source rather than from a shortened social-media graphic.

Meaning of the three phrases

SubhanAllah: Glory be to Allah. Alhamdulillah: All praise is for Allah. Allahu Akbar: Allah is the Greatest. Remembering the meanings can help keep the practice thoughtful rather than mechanical. The phrase “La ilaha illallah” is another important declaration of faith and appears in other narrated ways of completing a set of remembrance.

Why do some post-salah routines have different counts?

Hadith reports describe more than one form of remembrance after prayer. One well-known form is 33 SubhanAllah, 33 Alhamdulillah and 34 Allahu Akbar. Other reports describe saying each phrase 33 times and then completing the total with a tahlil declaration. These should be presented as distinct reported forms, not merged into one sequence. If you follow a particular school or teacher, ask them which narration and method they recommend.

Dhikr after salah in Arabic, transliteration and translation

When learning zikr after namaz, use a source that provides the Arabic text, a careful transliteration and a clear translation. Latin spellings are only pronunciation aids and can vary. If you are still learning Arabic, listen to a reliable recitation and practise the wording slowly. Avoid relying on a transliteration alone when a letter or vowel could change the pronunciation.

Frequently asked questions

Is 33-33-34 the only tasbih after salah?

No. Other authentic narrations describe different ways of completing the post-prayer remembrance, including 33 of each phrase followed by a tahlil phrase to complete 100.

Should Astaghfirullah be said after salah?

Sahih Muslim 591 reports seeking forgiveness three times after finishing prayer, followed by the supplication beginning Allahumma antas-salam.
