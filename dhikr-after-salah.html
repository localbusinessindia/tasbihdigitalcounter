What to say after the salam

A narration from Thawban says that when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ finished prayer, he sought forgiveness three times and then said: Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam, tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram. See Sahih Muslim 591.

The 33-33-34 tasbih after salah

DhikrArabicCountMeaning
SubhanAllahسُبْحَانَ اللَّٰهِ33Glory be to Allah
Alhamdulillahالْحَمْدُ لِلَّٰهِ33All praise is for Allah
Allahu Akbarاللَّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ34Allah is the Greatest

This 33-33-34 sequence is reported in Sahih Muslim 596a. It totals 100 repetitions. The 34 count belongs to Allahu Akbar in this post-prayer narration.

Another authentic way to complete 100

There is also a narration in Sahih Muslim 597a that mentions saying SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times and Allahu Akbar 33 times, then completing 100 with La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah.... These are distinct narrated wordings. This page’s guided counter follows the 33-33-34 version from Sahih Muslim 596a.

How to use the Sunnah 33-33-34 counter

  1. Open the guided counter with the button above.
  2. Tap once for each recitation of SubhanAllah; it moves to Alhamdulillah after 33.
  3. Tap 33 times for Alhamdulillah; it then moves to Allahu Akbar.
  4. Complete 34 repetitions of Allahu Akbar. The counter will show that the routine is complete.

You can also count on your fingers. A digital counter can keep the sequence organised; try to recite at a measured pace and stay mindful of the words.

Do not confuse the post-salah and bedtime routines

The post-salah version begins with SubhanAllah 33, then Alhamdulillah 33, then Allahu Akbar 34. A separate bedtime narration starts with Allahu Akbar 34, SubhanAllah 33 and Alhamdulillah 33. Read the bedtime dhikr guide for that separate sequence.

Frequently asked questions

Is 33-33-34 the only tasbih after salah?

No. Other authentic narrations describe different ways of completing the post-prayer remembrance, including 33 of each phrase followed by a tahlil phrase to complete 100.

Should Astaghfirullah be said after salah?

Sahih Muslim 591 reports seeking forgiveness three times after finishing prayer, followed by the supplication beginning Allahumma antas-salam.
