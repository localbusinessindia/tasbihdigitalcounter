What Does Astaghfirullah Mean? Arabic, English and Usage

Learn what Astaghfirullah means, how it is written in Arabic, when Muslims say it and how to build a steady istighfar habit with a digital tasbih counter.

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّٰهَ

Astaghfirullah (also written astagfirullah) means “I seek forgiveness from Allah.” It is a short phrase of istighfar: asking Allah to forgive our sins, mistakes and shortcomings.

Transliteration: As-tagh-fi-rul-lah. Pronunciation varies slightly across languages.

Count Astaghfirullah · Goal 100

What does Astaghfirullah mean?

The phrase combines a request for forgiveness with an acknowledgement that forgiveness is sought from Allah. Muslims may say it after making a mistake, after salah, during personal supplication or whenever they remember their need for Allah’s mercy. Saying it thoughtfully is more important than rushing through a large number.

Astaghfirullah after salah

After completing the prayer, the Prophet ﷺ would seek forgiveness three times and then say a supplication beginning Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam. This is reported in Sahih Muslim 591. The three repetitions are a specific post-prayer practice; they should not be confused with a universal daily limit.

Sayyid al-Istighfar: the longer supplication

Sayyid al-Istighfar is a longer supplication that begins Allahumma anta Rabbi, la ilaha illa anta (“O Allah, You are my Lord; there is no deity worthy of worship except You”). The narration describes it as the most superior way of asking Allah for forgiveness and mentions reciting it by day or night with sincere faith. Read the full wording and translation in Sahih al-Bukhari 6306.

A related narration: The Prophet ﷺ said that he sought Allah’s forgiveness and repented to Him more than seventy times a day. See Sahih al-Bukhari 6307. This encourages frequent repentance; a chosen counter goal is a personal aid, not a new religious obligation.

Astaghfirullah al-Azeem

A commonly used longer wording is Astaghfirullaha al-Azeem, meaning “I seek forgiveness from Allah, the Magnificent.” The essential meaning remains a request for Allah’s forgiveness. For a specific longer prophetic supplication, use the full wording of Sayyid al-Istighfar rather than assuming every longer phrase has the same source.

Benefits of seeking forgiveness

The Qur’an describes Prophet Nuh (peace be upon him) calling his people to seek forgiveness from their Lord and mentions blessings in the following verses (Qur’an 71:10–12). The passage is a reminder of Allah’s mercy and provision. It should not be reduced to a promise that repeating a phrase a fixed number of times guarantees a particular worldly result.

Istighfar can help a person acknowledge mistakes, turn back to Allah and maintain humility. Make the words meaningful, regret wrongdoing, and try to correct harm where possible.

When do Muslims say Astaghfirullah?

Astaghfirullah may be said when a person recognises a mistake, seeks Allah’s forgiveness, turns back in repentance or remembers their need for mercy. It is also part of specific reported practices. For example, the Prophet ﷺ sought forgiveness three times after completing the prayer; see Sahih Muslim 591. This post-prayer count should be kept distinct from personal daily goals.

Astaghfirullah spelling and transliteration variants

Common English spellings include Astaghfirullah, Astagfirullah, Astaghfiru Allah and Astaghfirullāh. These are attempts to represent the same Arabic phrase أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّٰهَ. Transliteration conventions differ, so a spelling variation does not necessarily indicate a different supplication. For accurate recitation, use the Arabic text and learn the pronunciation from a trusted teacher.

Astaghfirullah meaning in Urdu and Hindi

Urdu: “میں اللہ سے مغفرت طلب کرتا/کرتی ہوں۔”

Hindi: “मैं अल्लाह से माफ़ी माँगता/माँगती हूँ।”

In simple English: “I seek forgiveness from Allah.” The phrase expresses a request for forgiveness; the wording itself does not remove the need to stop wrongdoing and make amends where appropriate.

How to build a steady istighfar habit

  • Choose a regular moment, such as after salah or during a quiet break.
  • Say the phrase at a comfortable pace and reflect on its meaning.
  • If you set a goal such as 33 or 100, treat it as a personal consistency target unless you are following a specific sourced practice.
  • When repentance involves harm to another person, take practical steps to put matters right where possible.

How many times should you say Astaghfirullah?

There is no single compulsory count for every occasion. The Sunnah includes saying it three times after salah, while the Prophet ﷺ also sought forgiveness frequently throughout the day. You may set a manageable personal goal such as 33 or 100 on a digital counter to support consistency, while remembering that the number is your tracking target unless it is tied to a specific sourced practice.

Astaghfirullah meaning in daily life

Astaghfirullah is often said after recognising a mistake, when seeking forgiveness, or as part of regular remembrance. The phrase is short, so it can be included in a quiet daily routine at home, while travelling or during a break. Its purpose is not merely to repeat a sound a certain number of times; it is to turn sincerely to Allah, acknowledge shortcomings and seek His forgiveness.

Astaghfirullah after prayer and at other times

A well-known narration in Sahih Muslim reports that the Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) sought forgiveness three times after completing the prayer, then said a supplication beginning “Allahumma antas-salam.” This is a specific post-prayer practice. Beyond that, a Muslim may make istighfar at other times as part of personal remembrance. Keep the evidence for a prescribed count separate from a personal goal chosen simply to build consistency.

Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirallah and Astaghfurillah: spelling variations

Searches may use spellings such as Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirallah, Astaghfurillah, Astagfirullah or Astaghfiru Allah. These are attempts to represent the Arabic phrase in Latin letters. Transliteration is not perfectly standardised, so a spelling variation does not always mean the speaker intends a different phrase. For accurate pronunciation, refer to the Arabic text and a reliable audio recitation.

Astaghfirullah meaning in Urdu and Hindi

In Urdu, the meaning can be expressed as “Main Allah se maghfirat talab karta/karti hoon” — I seek forgiveness from Allah. In simple Hindi, it means “Main Allah se maafi maangta/maangti hoon.” The wording in English is commonly given as “I seek forgiveness from Allah.” The Arabic phrase is the original wording; translations explain its meaning but cannot capture every nuance of the Arabic expression.

How to build an istighfar habit without rushing

Choose a manageable time, such as after salah or during a quiet moment in the day. Say the phrase attentively, understand what you are asking for and avoid treating a high counter total as a measure of sincerity. If you use a digital counter, set a personal goal only as a practical reminder. The counter can record repetitions, but it cannot measure intention, repentance or acceptance.

Related phrases and longer supplications

Astaghfirullah is a short form of seeking forgiveness. Some Muslims also learn longer supplications, including Sayyid al-Istighfar, from recognised hadith collections. Because longer duas have specific wording, read them from a trusted Arabic source with a checked translation. Do not combine fragments from different supplications or assume every longer version has the same wording or source.

Frequently asked questions

What does Astaghfirullah mean in English?

It means “I seek forgiveness from Allah.”

Is Astaghfirullah the same as istighfar?

Astaghfirullah is a phrase used to seek forgiveness. Istighfar is the broader act of asking Allah for forgiveness.
