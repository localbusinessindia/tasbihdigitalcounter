Online tasbih counter features for everyday dhikr

This free online tasbih counter is designed for people who want a simple way to count tasbih, tasbeeh, zikr or dhikr on a phone or computer. The large digital counter makes each tap easy to see, while the progress ring gives a quick visual indication of how close you are to a chosen target. You can use the tool for a short set of 33 repetitions, a 99-count session, a 100-count personal target or a custom number that suits your routine.

Unlike a basic tally counter, the page includes common Arabic remembrance phrases with transliteration and a short meaning. Select SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illallah, Astaghfirullah or another available dhikr. The counter is a counting aid: read and pronounce the words carefully, and keep your attention on the remembrance rather than on the screen.

Digital tasbih, tasbeeh counter and zikr counter

People search for this kind of tool using different spellings, including digital tasbih counter, digital tasbeeh counter, online tasbeeh, online zikr counter, tesbih counter and dhikr counter online. These phrases often describe the same basic need: a convenient way to keep track of repeated remembrance. This site focuses on Islamic dhikr counting rather than general-purpose number tallying.

Use the counter with or without a target

Choose one of the preset goals or select the infinity option to count without a fixed finish point. If you are following a personal routine, the custom goal lets you enter another number. Undo corrects an accidental tap, while Reset starts the selected phrase again. Counts are stored locally in the browser on the device, and the export option lets you save a backup file. Because browser storage can be cleared, export a backup if you want to preserve your totals.

Does an online tasbih counter replace prayer beads?

No. A digital tasbih counter is simply another counting option. Some people count on their fingers, some use prayer beads (misbaha or tasbeeh), and others prefer a phone-based counter. The important point is to use a method that helps you keep track without distracting you from the words and their meaning. Follow the practice and counting method taught by the scholar or source you trust.

Privacy, offline use and device support

The counter does not require an account. Your counts and preferences are saved in local browser storage, not in a personal online profile. After the site has been opened once and its files have been cached, the counter may remain available offline on supported browsers. Vibration depends on device and browser support; many iPhones and some desktop browsers do not expose the web vibration feature, so the sound option can be used as an alternative where available.

Frequently asked questions

What is an online tasbih counter?

An online tasbih counter is a digital way to keep track of repeated dhikr. Tap once for each recitation instead of keeping the number in your head.

Are tasbih and tasbeeh the same?

They are common English spellings of the same Arabic word. People may also use “tasbih” to refer to prayer beads or a digital counter.

What is the difference between the after-salah and bedtime modes?

After salah, the guided routine is SubhanAllah 33, Alhamdulillah 33 and Allahu Akbar 34. Before sleep, the separate routine is Allahu Akbar 34, SubhanAllah 33 and Alhamdulillah 33.

Does the counter save my progress?

Yes. Counts, daily totals and settings are saved locally in your browser on your device. You can export a JSON backup from the counter.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit, the pages and counter are cached by your browser. You can then continue counting when you are offline, subject to your browser's storage settings.

Is this digital tasbih counter free?

Yes. The counter is free to use. The website may show ads to support its operation.
