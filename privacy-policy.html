Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Tasbih Digital Counter (the "site") handles information when you visit https://tasbihdigitalcounter.netlify.app/.

Data stored on your device

Your counts, goals, streaks, custom dhikr and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we do not have user accounts.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name or email to use the counter. Like most sites, our hosting provider (Netlify) may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested for security and operation.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense and other partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

No advertising or advertising cookies are active on the site at the time of this update. If advertising is enabled, the site will load advertising scripts only after you choose to allow them in a consent prompt, and your choice is stored on your device (local storage key “tdc_consent”). Where you decline, the counter works exactly the same and no advertising script is requested. If ads are added, this policy will be updated first, and where the law requires it a consent choice will be offered before advertising cookies are set (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland).

Analytics

The site does not currently run analytics. If privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated first.

Offline files (service worker)

To let the counter and guides work offline, the site installs a service worker that stores copies of its own pages and images in your browser's cache storage. These copies contain no personal data and are replaced when the site is updated. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can clear saved counter data at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions? See our contact page.
