About Tasbih Digital Counter

Tasbih Digital Counter is a free online tasbih (tasbeeh) and dhikr counter for people who want a simple way to keep count on a phone, tablet or computer. The counter does not require an account or app installation. It stores counting data in the browser on the device being used.

What this website provides

The site includes a flexible counter, guided routines for selected remembrances, and educational pages explaining the meaning and context of common Arabic phrases. You will also find introductory material about morning and evening adhkar, remembrance after salah, bedtime dhikr, the 99 Names of Allah and Islamic dates. Our aim is to make the tool easy to use and the supporting information easy to navigate.

Editorial approach and sources

Religious content should be handled carefully. Where a page discusses a specific practice, we aim to link directly to the relevant Qur’anic passage or hadith collection and identify the cited reference. Hadith numbering and translations can differ between editions and websites, so readers should check the linked source rather than relying on a number alone. We do not issue fatwas or present this website as a substitute for a qualified scholar.

Who writes and reviews the content?

Pages are prepared and maintained by the Tasbih Digital Counter editorial team. At present, we do not claim review by a named Islamic scholar or hadith specialist. References are provided so readers can inspect the source material themselves. If a qualified reviewer is formally engaged in the future, their name, qualifications and review scope should be stated here accurately.

Corrections and updates

We welcome corrections, especially where a translation, transliteration, hadith reference or description of a religious practice needs clarification. When reporting an issue, please include the page URL and the source you believe should be checked. We may update a page when a correction is supported by a reliable reference.
