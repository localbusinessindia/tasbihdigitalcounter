Why Muslim holidays may vary by country

The Hijri calendar is lunar. Ramadan begins and the two Eids are observed according to the relevant moon-sighting or official calendar process used by each community. Therefore, the date in one country can differ from the date announced in another country. Always confirm with your local mosque or recognised religious authority before making travel, work or family plans.

How the Islamic calendar works

The Islamic calendar has twelve lunar months. Its year is shorter than the Gregorian solar year, so Ramadan and other Muslim holidays move earlier by around ten or eleven days in the Gregorian calendar each year. This is why searches such as “Muslim holidays 2027”, “Ramadan 2027 dates”, “Eid dates” and “Islamic calendar 2027” are year-specific.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official dates for every country?

No. They are Umm al-Qura-based planning estimates. Local authorities may confirm a date one day earlier or later based on the method followed.

When is the next Eid in 2027?

On the Umm al-Qura estimate shown here, Eid al-Fitr is 9 March 2027 and Eid al-Adha is 16 May 2027. Confirm locally before relying on either date.

Why does the Hijri date change after sunset?

In the Islamic calendar, the new day begins at sunset. A browser date display generally formats the current civil date; local observance may follow the sunset boundary.
