# After publishing: quick checks (about 10 minutes, on your phone)
- [ ] Home page opens, tap the big circle: the number goes up.
- [ ] Open the "Guided routine player", start "After salah", tap 33 times: it moves to step 2. Close the page, reopen: "Unfinished routine" appears.
- [ ] Open /subhanallah, press "Count this dhikr" (or the start link): counter opens with SubhanAllah, goal 33, and a "Back to guide" link.
- [ ] Open /astaghfirullah, enter 50, press Start counting: counter shows "of 50".
- [ ] Open /99-names-of-allah: search for "rahim", star a name, reload: the star stays.
- [ ] Press Listen on an Arabic text: you should see a message saying it is the device voice, not a recording.
- [ ] Open /ar and /ur: text runs right-to-left and the page does not scroll sideways.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode after one visit: the home page still opens.
- [ ] https://tasbihdigitalcounter.netlify.app/sitemap.xml opens and lists 27 addresses.
- [ ] Android only: switch Vibration on and tap; tell me if the phone does not vibrate.
