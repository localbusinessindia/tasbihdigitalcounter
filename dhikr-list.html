Dhikr List with Arabic, Transliteration and Meaning

Pick a phrase, read the Arabic, check the meaning and open the free tasbih counter with that dhikr already selected. The starting counts below are common personal targets used for tracking, not rulings.

This list is a counting aid. Wording, counts and times for specific remembrances can differ between authentic narrations, so follow the source or teacher you trust for what to recite and how many times. Where a phrase comes from the Quran, the verse is shown in its meaning. See the disclaimer and the guides for after salah, before sleep and morning and evening.

Glory and praise be to Allah; glory be to Allah, the Almighty.

SubhanAllah wal-hamdulillah wa la ilaha illallah wallahu akbar

Istighfar: seeking forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ ٱللَّٰهَ

Astaghfirullah

I seek forgiveness from Allah.

Count this dhikr · Goal 100
أَسْتَغْفِرُ ٱللَّٰهَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

Astaghfirullaha wa atubu ilayh

I seek Allah's forgiveness and turn to Him in repentance.

Count this dhikr · Goal 100
أَسْتَغْفِرُ ٱللَّٰهَ ٱلْعَظِيمَ ٱلَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَيَّ ٱلْقَيُّومَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

Astaghfirullah al-Azeem alladhi la ilaha illa huwal Hayyul Qayyumu wa atubu ilayh

I seek forgiveness from Allah the Almighty, there is no god but Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer, and I turn to Him in repentance.

Count this dhikr · Goal 3
رَبِّ ٱغْفِرْ لِي

Rabbi ighfir li

My Lord, forgive me.

Count this dhikr · Goal 100
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ ٱلْعَفْوَ فَٱعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka afuwwun tuhibbul afwa fa'fu anni

O Allah, You are Pardoning and love to pardon, so pardon me.

Count this dhikr · Goal 33

Duas and remembrance from the Quran and Sunnah

حَسْبُنَا ٱللَّٰهُ وَنِعْمَ ٱلْوَكِيلُ

Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel

Allah is enough for us, the best disposer of affairs.

Count this dhikr · Goal 33
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّالِمِينَ

La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin

There is no god but You; glory be to You; indeed I was among the wrongdoers (Quran 21:87).

Count this dhikr · Goal 100
حَسْبِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ

Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa huwa alayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa Rabbul Arshil Azeem

Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but Him; in Him I trust, and He is Lord of the Mighty Throne (Quran 9:129).

Count this dhikr · Goal 33
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي ٱلْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina adhaban-nar

Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire (Quran 2:201).

Count this dhikr · Goal 33
يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ

Ya Hayyu ya Qayyum, bi rahmatika astaghith

O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, in Your mercy I seek help.

Count this dhikr · Goal 100
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ أَنتَ ٱلسَّلَامُ وَمِنكَ ٱلسَّلَامُ تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا ٱلْجَلَالِ وَٱلْإِكْرَامِ

Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam, tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram

O Allah, You are Peace and from You comes peace; blessed are You, Possessor of majesty and honour.

Count this dhikr · No limit
